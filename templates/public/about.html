About {{ SITE_NAME }}

We are a South African micro-lender built on transparency, affordability, and modern financial technology.

Our purpose

To provide access to fair, transparent credit to South Africans who need it — without compromising on responsible lending standards.

What makes us different

  • Affordability first. We use a deterministic engine to verify what you can responsibly repay.
  • AI where it helps — never where it decides. AI assists our human reviewers. It does not approve or reject loans.
  • Transparent pricing. Interest and fees are shown upfront, in Rand, in writing.
  • Data minimisation. We collect only what we need, and we retain it according to POPIA requirements.
