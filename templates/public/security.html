Security

Security is foundational to {{ SITE_NAME }}. Here's how we protect your information and our platform.

How we protect your data

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ with HSTS.
  • Data at rest is protected by disk encryption and access controls.
  • Documents are stored privately and served only via authenticated backend downloads.
  • Strict role-based access control is enforced server-side on every request.
  • All sensitive actions are written to an immutable audit log.

How we protect your account

  • Strong password policy and rate-limited login.
  • Account lockout after repeated failed attempts.
  • Continuous monitoring for suspicious activity.
  • Session expiry and session management.

Reporting a vulnerability

Please email {{ SITE_SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. We will respond promptly and will not take legal action against good-faith security researchers.
