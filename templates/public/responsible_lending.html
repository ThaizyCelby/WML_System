Responsible lending

{{ SITE_NAME }} is committed to lending responsibly. We will never approve a loan we do not believe you can afford to repay.

How we assess affordability

Every application is assessed using a deterministic engine that considers:

  • Your verified gross monthly income.
  • Your essential living expenses.
  • Your existing debt obligations.
  • The proposed monthly repayment.
  • Your disposable income after all of the above.

What we will not do

  • We will not approve a loan that leaves you without reasonable disposable income.
  • We will not charge hidden fees or apply retroactive interest changes.
  • We will not use AI as the sole authority for a lending decision.
  • We will not use protected characteristics to discriminate.
  • We will not harass you if you fall behind — we will work with you.

If you're struggling to repay

Contact us before you miss a payment. We may be able to:

  • Adjust your collection date within your collection window.
  • Arrange a restructuring or payment plan.
  • Refer you to a registered debt counsellor.

Email {{ SITE_SUPPORT_EMAIL }} or call {{ SITE_SUPPORT_PHONE }}.

Over-indebtedness

If you believe you are over-indebted, you have the right under the NCA to apply to a debt counsellor for a formal debt review. We will cooperate fully with that process.

Your rights under the NCA

  • The right to apply for credit.
  • The right to be given reasons for a credit decision.
  • The right to receive a clear, written quotation before accepting.
  • The right to confidentiality.
  • The right to access and challenge credit records.
