Accessibility

We are committed to making {{ SITE_NAME }} usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML and correct heading hierarchy.
  • Keyboard-navigable interfaces with visible focus indicators.
  • ARIA labels on interactive components.
  • Sufficient colour contrast in both light and dark mode.
  • Respect for the operating-system "reduced motion" setting.
  • Responsive layouts that work down to small mobile screens.
  • Form fields with associated labels and inline error messages.

Known limitations

  • Some older PDF documents we generate may not be fully tagged for screen readers. If you need an accessible version, email us.
  • Bank statement parsing relies on text extraction; scanned image PDFs may need manual review.

Tell us about a problem

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please email {{ SITE_SUPPORT_EMAIL }} with:

  • The page or feature you were using.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • The assistive technology you use, if any.

We will respond within five business days and aim to fix confirmed issues within thirty days.
